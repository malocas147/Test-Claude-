FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Send up to 5 invoices a month with payment links, forever, with no card required. You only pay standard processing fees when a client pays you.

How fast do I receive money?

Card payments typically arrive in your bank account within 2 business days; bank transfers depend on your region. Halcyon never sits on your funds.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open a secure link, review the invoice and pay. They can optionally save a payment method for future invoices.

Can I use my own branding?

On Pro and Studio you can add your logo, colors and a custom email domain. Studio also removes Halcyon branding entirely.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access until the end of your billing period, then drop to the free plan. Export every invoice and client record any time — your data is yours.
